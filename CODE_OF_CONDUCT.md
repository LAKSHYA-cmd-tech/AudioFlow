# Code of conduct

AudioFlow welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and code without personal attacks. Respect privacy, acknowledge others' work, and make room for differing perspectives.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable. Do not post another person's private information or unwanted contact details.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project community spaces, including representation of the project elsewhere.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or ban participants according to the severity and repetition of the behavior. Decisions should be proportional and consider context.

Use GitHub's reporting tools for abuse. For a private concern about project participation, request a private maintainer contact without disclosing private details publicly. Do not repeat harmful material in an issue.
